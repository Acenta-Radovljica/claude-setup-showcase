# Example: How to format your questions file

Below is a sample input. Copy this structure for your own surveys.
The skill reads this file and turns it into a complete HTML survey.

---

# Digital Marketing Health Check
Subtitle: How mature is your digital marketing? Answer honestly -- there are no wrong answers.

## 1. Strategy & Goals
- We have clearly defined marketing goals for this quarter.
- Our marketing strategy is documented and shared with the team.
- We regularly review and adjust our strategy based on data.
- We know exactly who our target audience is.

## 2. Content & Social Media
- We publish content on a consistent schedule.
- Our content is tailored to our target audience.
- We repurpose content across multiple channels.
- We track engagement metrics (likes, shares, comments).

## 3. SEO & Website
- Our website loads in under 3 seconds on mobile.
- We regularly publish blog content optimized for search.
- We track keyword rankings for our main terms.
- Our website has clear calls-to-action on every page.

## 4. Paid Advertising
- We run paid campaigns with clear ROI targets.
- We A/B test our ad creatives regularly.
- We use retargeting to reach past visitors.
- Our cost per acquisition is within target.

## 5. Analytics & Reporting
- We have Google Analytics (or similar) properly configured.
- We review marketing data at least weekly.
- We can attribute revenue to specific marketing channels.
- We use data to make decisions, not gut feeling.

## Scoring
Scale: 1 (Strongly disagree) to 5 (Strongly agree)

URL: YOUR_WEBHOOK_URL_HERE
